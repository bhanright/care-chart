# Logo concepts

Eight concepts generated on 2026-10-09 through OpenRouter (`openai/gpt-5.4-image-2` and
`google/gemini-3-pro-image`), from four prompts: heart-pulse, clipboard-check, care-hands
and pill-plus. `contact-sheet.png` shows them side by side.

The chosen logo is `pill-plus--openai.png` (the gradient cross with a white heart); its
cleaned-up, transparent version is `public/images/logo.png`, and the app icons
`public/icon-192.png` and `public/icon-512.png` are made from it. The others are kept in
case they are wanted later.
